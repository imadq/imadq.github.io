AI analysis of customer reviews, pulled through the marketplace APIs

Reviews arrive by the thousand and nobody has time to read them all. A scheduled job pulls new reviews through the marketplace APIs, a language model tags each one by theme and sentiment against a fixed taxonomy, and the results land in the warehouse next to sales, so a rating drop shows up beside the return rate for the same product.

  • Only new reviews are scored; nothing is re-run or double counted

03 — Automation & AI

Find the manual step. Then make it disappear.

The same four moves every time, whether the source is a marketplace API, a mailbox full of PDFs, or a folder of product photos. Language and vision models go in the middle where rules break down; plain code everywhere else.

01

Find the manual step

Where do the hours go? Usually exporting, copying and re-checking the same numbers. That step becomes the spec.

02

Wire the source

APIs, scheduled queries, scrapes or mailbox polls, written once with retries and logging, and run by a scheduler instead of a person.

03

Model and check

Data lands in the warehouse, gets modeled into the tables people actually ask questions of, and passes tests before anything downstream refreshes.

04

Report and alert

Dashboards for the decisions, scheduled emails and shared-drive exports for the people who live in Outlook and Excel, and a short alert for the exceptions. If nothing broke, nobody has to open anything.
